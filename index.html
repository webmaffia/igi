Natural diamonds form deep underground over millions of years. Lab-grown diamonds form in weeks. Chemically and optically they are the same. So how can you tell them apart?
NATURAL DIAMOND
FORMED
BY TIME
Created deep within the Earth under intense heat and pressure over millions of years.
LAB-GROWN DIAMOND
GROWN

Created deep within the Earth under intense heat and pressure over millions of years.
LAB-GROWN DIAMOND
Created
by innovation
Grown with advanced technology that replicates nature’s own process, lab-grown diamonds share the same physical, chemical and optical properties as natural ones. Modern in origin, identical in composition, and made to shine just as beautifully.

NATURAL DIAMOND
Shaped
by nature
Born deep within the Earth, natural diamonds form over billions of years under extraordinary heat and pressure. Rare, enduring and timeless, each carries a story shaped by nature — and no two are exactly alike.

Step one
It arrives
without a name.
Every stone is logged anonymously. Our graders never know who sent it — so nothing but the stone itself can influence the result.
Step two
We ask where
it came from.
Advanced screening instruments confirm whether a diamond is natural or laboratory-grown, and detect treatments invisible to the eye.
Step three
Several experts.
One standard.
Multiple gemologists independently assess the 4Cs — Carat, Color, Clarity and Cut — under controlled lighting and magnification.
Step four
A signature,
in light.
A microscopic laser inscription on the girdle links the stone permanently to its report — invisible to the eye, readable under a loupe.
Step five
Its story,
on record.
A secure IGI report is issued and stored online — so anyone, anywhere, can verify it in seconds.
